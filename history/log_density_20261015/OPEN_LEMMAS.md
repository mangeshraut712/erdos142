## L1. Logarithmic-density completion packet existence

Retain the exact admissibility, rank, and variance hypotheses of
`THREE_BRANCH_AMPLIFICATION_20261014.md` Theorem 4. In the completion branch,
prove positive retained pairing S_gamma for a nonnegative channel combination,
and find s frequencies capturing at least a fixed theta fraction, with

    s <= theta gamma/(w sqrt(c)) a^(-1/4)ell(a)^(-1/2).

Then a single coefficient reaches c a^(5/2)ell(a), and its quarter-arc
refinement gains at least kappa^2 times that squared coefficient. A packet
of O(a^(-1/4)/ell(a)) would suffice, but is not established. Moment weighting
requires its actual effective density S_(m+1)/S_m; no lower bound a/2 is free.
See `RESIDUAL_FIELD_AUDIT_20261015.md` (A1)–(A3).

The sparse theorem (A6)–(A7) rules out dropping the logarithmic-density
hypothesis: actual AP-free sets at a comparable to p^(-3/5) fail this
capture property even for the trivial factor and actual completion fields.
It is not a counterexample in the required regime.

## L2. Branch II concentration, including the cubic-field alternative

If |T_u+S_u|>=c_1 a^3, the sufficient winning-shell cutoff is
s <= c_1/(32 C_J c^(3/2)) a^(-3/4)ell(a)^(-5/2).
Prove that an adequate small shell exists, or extract a different sufficient
increment from a large shell. When that cubic scalar is small, the pairing
with K_u^(3) remains a separate unresolved case. Do not infer a per-character
increment from large total spectral energy.

## L3. A stable quadratic increment architecture

`QUADRATIC_LOCALIZATION_20261015.md` proves a<=3/4+o(1) for one quadratic
interval host, with explicit errors. Extend the localized weighted count
and degree bounds to the actual intersections generated by a specified
quadratic refinement. Prove correlation, density gain, retained mass,
complexity, cumulative losses, and terminal eligibility. The old linear
rank/size ledger does not automatically apply to quadratic intersections.

## L4. Proper-window quantitative reductions

Full-group pair independence, the D_3 lower bound, and full-group branch
elimination require independent estimates on proper windows. Historical
proper-window error formulas alone do not show their errors are o(a^4)
uniformly over the full rank and window-size range. Check those comparisons
before using a two-branch reduction in L1 or L2.

All four entries are OPEN. No target-density counterexample has been found.
