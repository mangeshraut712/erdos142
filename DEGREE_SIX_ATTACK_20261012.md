## 2. The canonical extension and all cardinality-preserving corrections

Put T_r(S)=Σ_{Q subset S, |Q|=4} t(Q). Counting the new point and then
using the triple balance gives the exact identity

    Σ_{x outside S} T_{r+1}(S union {x})
                     =(n-r-4) T_r(S).                   (21)

    m_5^0(S)=ρ_5[1+α_5 T_5(S)], α_5=(n-4)/(n-8),
    m_6^0(S)=ρ_6[1+α_6 T_6(S)],

It satisfies cardinality through degree five exactly. Calling this the
canonical extension does not mean every extension must have this form.

## 3. First failure: five overlapping AP positions

    P_n=96/(n-4)+192n/[(n-4)(n-5)(n-6)]<1/4.

violates five-variable probability nonnegativity.

Thus the unchanged harmonic lift is decisively refuted, with a deficit
of order a^5. Any valid correction z_5 must satisfy z_5(S)>ρ_5/4 on these
five-point progressions, together with the zero down-sums in (23).
Proper interval H windows contain these configurations, so this is not
an objection confined to a geometrically ineligible example.

This is an exact obstruction to (22). It is not an inconsistency in the
October 12 fourth moments and does not exclude a nonzero z_5 correction.

## 4. A degree-six SOS certificate valid for every AP-free Boolean set

Fix v in Ω, let D_v be the nonzero differences whose four positions
v,v+d,v+2d,v+3d lie in Ω, put m=|D_v|, and define the cubic polynomial

    H_v=Σ_{d in D_v} X_{v+d}X_{v+2d}X_{v+3d}.

For a genuine AP-free Boolean configuration, X_v H_v=0. For every real s,
Boolean reduction gives the polynomial certificate

    H_v²-2sH_v+s²(1-X_v)
       =[H_v-s(1-X_v)]²-2s X_v H_v >=0                  (25)

modulo the AP equalities. The square has degree six, and the AP term has
degree four. This is an explicit symbolic certificate, not a numerical
infeasibility report.

Every degree-six PSD extension of the audited moments must therefore have

    L(H_v²) >= [L(H_v)]²/(1-a)
             =m²ρ_3²/(1-a),                            (26)

by taking s=mρ_3/(1-a). Only existing third moments and AP fourth moments
are used in the last equality. Equivalently, the degree-six quadratic
form of H_v and 1-X_v must be positive semidefinite.

## 5. Forced sixth-order correlations: quantitative new constraint

Let T_d={v+d,v+2d,v+3d}. For any fixed d there are at most nine e with
T_d intersect T_e nonempty: equality id=je determines e for each
1<=i,j<=3. Thus at most 9m ordered pairs overlap.

Cubic PSD and Boolean reduction give
|L(X_{T_d}X_{T_e})|<=sqrt(L(X_{T_d}²)L(X_{T_e}²))=ρ_3.
Thus every overlapping-pair union moment is at most ρ_3; no additional
local probability assumption is needed. Combining this with (26), the sum over
disjoint pairs is at least

    m²ρ_3²/(1-a)-9mρ_3.                                 (27)

For k>=6, ρ_3>=a³/2. If m a^4>=36, then 9ρ_3/m<=aρ_3²/2.
There are at most m² disjoint ordered pairs. Consequently their average
sixth moment obeys

    average_{T_d disjoint T_e} L(X_{T_d union T_e})
         >= ρ_3²(1+a/2)
         >= ρ_6+a^7/8.                                 (28)

Here ρ_6<=ρ_3² follows directly from the falling factorial ratios.
All divisions are legitimate: these hypotheses make the right side
positive and imply there are disjoint pairs.

This exposes a new constraint: AP exclusion forces a positive correlation
of order a^7 between disjoint triples completing the same vertex. Merely
retaining the uniform sixth moments, or perturbing them by O(a^6/n),
cannot satisfy it when n a is large. The relative enhancement is order a.

## 7. A degree-eight global certificate and direct analytic route

There is also a global identity. On a fixed-size AP-free Boolean set,

    (n-k) Σ_v H_v²-(Σ_v H_v)²

To verify it, expand the squares, use X_v H_v=0 and X_v²=X_v, and then
Σ_v(1-X_v)=n-k. Each square has degree at most eight. Thus (31) is a
valid degree-eight certificate of a global completion-energy inequality.
It does not construct a degree-eight extension; feasibility of degree six

## 8. A rigorous global-extension endpoint at growing degree

There is an exact, but expensive, realizability criterion. Suppose moments
through degree k have all m_k(S)>=0, normalized m_0=1, and the cardinality
identities through degree k-1. Repeatedly summing the identities gives

    Σ_{|S|=k, S containing T} m_k(S)=m_|T|(T), |T|<=k.

Each k-set is counted (k-|T|)! times in the ordered summation; the product
of the cardinality factors is the same factorial. Hence μ(S)=m_k(S)
is a global probability law on k-subsets representing ALL those moments.
If AP fourth moments are zero, nonnegativity shows μ is supported on
AP-free k-subsets.

If a full preordering is available through degree k+2, with cardinality
and nonnegative local laws, all moments of sets larger than k vanish.
For size k+1 this follows by summing nonnegative m_{T union {i}} at a
k-set T; the cardinality right side is zero. Monotonicity extends this to
size k+2. The cap times the literal product X_S now gives

    0<=L[(τ-|c_ξ|²)X_S]=(τ-|c_ξ(S)|²) μ(S).

Thus every S with μ(S)>0 satisfies every cap pointwise. A genuine global
solution follows. This proves an endpoint for the hierarchy, without
asserting that degree six or eight suffices.

At k~n/log p this endpoint has degree of that order and at least binomial
(n,k) top-level entries. It is quantitatively far from an efficient or
fixed-degree proof and supplies no target theorem by itself.

A fresh elementary random-alteration attempt also fails to certify the
required density. Sample each point of Z_p with probability b and remove
at most one point per surviving AP edge. Since the number of distinct
unordered edges is at most p(p-1)/2, the expected retained size is at least
pb-p(p-1)b^4/2. This guarantee is optimized at b³=1/[2(p-1)], giving
retained density (3/4)[2(p-1)]^(-1/3). At b~ε/log p the guarantee is
negative. This diagnoses that alteration estimate, not the impossibility
of better deletion methods or the nonexistence of denser AP-free sets.
It yields no counterexample meeting the density and spectral premises.

The finite actual AP-free enumeration in the verifier checks the universal
certificates, not an asymptotic counterexample. The five-point obstruction

The useful advance is precise: the first omitted AP multiplier defeats
