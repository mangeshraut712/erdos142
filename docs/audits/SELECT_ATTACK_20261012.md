## Consequence for the proposed proof route

cardinality, AP and Fourier-cap constraints cannot certify their
infeasibility in this regime. This statement has a precise scope: total
degree of each certificate term is at most four before Boolean reduction.
Apply the constructed functional to any proposed certificate of a negative
constant: equality terms vanish and every permitted nonnegative term has
nonnegative value, a contradiction.

continues to be a limitation of a specified proof system, not a global

not guarantee a simultaneous law on all n vertices. Higher-degree
