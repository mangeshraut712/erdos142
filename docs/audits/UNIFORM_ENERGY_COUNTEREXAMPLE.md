# The unrestricted uniform energy lemma is false

Date: 2026-09-14. Offline proof from the checkpoint's window definition.

STATUS: (B) ENERGY LEMMA FALSE

This status concerns the new statement quantified over **every controlled
window without a size/AP-availability cutoff**. It does not refute the
cutoff-qualified lemma in LOCALIZATION_BRIDGE.md, and it does not prove or
disprove r_4(N)=o(N/log N). No literature novelty or prize claim is made.

## 1. Exact statement proved

There is a deterministic sequence (p_d,Omega_d,A_d), d>=2, with p_d prime,
Omega_d a controlled narrow linear-coordinate window, A_d subset Omega_d,
and n_d=|Omega_d|=2^d, such that:

1. Omega_d itself is cyclic 3AP-free, hence A_d is cyclic 4AP-free.
2. a_d=|A_d|/n_d=floor(2^d/d)/2^d. In particular
   a_d log n_d -> log 2 and a_d log p_d -> log 64.
3. For every fixed C>0, uniformly over factors F generated by at most
   C log(2/a_d) characters divided into four arbitrarily shifted equal arcs,

       Var_{Omega_d}(E(1_{A_d}|F))/a_d^2 -> 0.

The same family works for any fixed number b>=2 of equal arcs per
character. Thus changing four to another fixed number does not repair
the unrestricted statement.

The construction of A_d is an explicit finite deterministic selection
rule. Its existence is proved probabilistically below; it is not an
abstract law offered in place of actual sets. No efficiency claim is made
for generating the enormous selected sets.

## 2. Controlled AP-free windows

Put D=8^d. Let p=p_d be the smallest prime exceeding 100D^2. The
checkpoint's prime-existence result gives

    100*64^d < p < 200*64^d.                                (1)

This is also an effective definition of p, by finite primality search.
For j=1,...,d let xi_j=ceil(p/8^j). Define Omega by the d+1 conditions

    0<=x<=D-1,
    0<=(xi_j x mod p)<=floor(3p/16), j=1,...,d.              (2)

All are single consecutive coordinate intervals with diameter below p/3;
all their frequencies are nonzero. Thus (2) is exactly a controlled
window of the checkpoint's class, of rank at most d+1.

In fact

    Omega={sum_{i=0}^{d-1} epsilon_i 8^i: epsilon_i in {0,1}}. (3)

To prove this, write x=sum v_i 8^i with 0<=v_i<=7. Since
0<xi_j-p/8^j<1, for 0<=x<D,

    xi_j x/p=x/8^j+e_j(x),  0<=e_j(x)<D/p<1/(100D).

Inductively assume the lower digits v_0,...,v_{j-2} are in {0,1}.
Their fractional contribution to x/8^j is below 1/56. If v_{j-1} is
0 or 1, the entire fractional part is at most 1/7, and adding e_j still
keeps it strictly below 3/16. If v_{j-1}>=2, that fractional part is
between 1/4 and 7/8+1/56=25/28. Adding e_j neither wraps it past one
nor lowers it to 3/16. Condition j in (2) therefore holds exactly for
v_{j-1} in {0,1}. The j=1 case has no lower digits and starts the induction.
Comparison with floor(3p/16) is exact because the coordinate residue is
an integer. This proves (3), hence |Omega|=2^d.

For a modular equation x+z=2y with x,y,z in Omega, its integer discrepancy
has magnitude at most 2(D-1)<p, so it is an integer equality. Adding two
base-eight digits from {0,1}, or doubling one, creates no carry. Each digit
therefore satisfies epsilon+epsilon'=2epsilon'', forcing all three
digits to coincide. Thus x=y=z. Omega has no nontrivial cyclic 3AP,
and every subset of it is cyclic 4AP-free.

## 3. A finite family contains every allowed quantized factor

Let s=floor(sqrt(d)). For each integer 2<=b<=d, consider all factors on
Omega formed from j<=s characters, each partitioned into b shifted
half-open equal arcs. Empty cells are omitted.

For one nonzero character, its labels on the p-point cyclic grid change
only when an arc boundary passes a grid point. All critical shifts have
the form t/(bp). Every possible labeling is represented by a shift
t/(2bp), 0<=t<2bp: these include critical shifts and points between them.
This counts boundary cases as well as generic offsets. The zero character
gives a constant label and causes no exception. Hence there are at most
2bp^2 labeled single-coordinate partitions.

For a fixed b, at most (s+1)(2bp^2)^s factors of at most s coordinates
occur, and each has at most b^s cells. Across all 2<=b<=d, the total
number T_d of (factor, nonempty cell) pairs is at most

    T_d <= d(s+1)(2d^2 p^2)^s.                             (4)

The count is an overestimate; duplicate characters and duplicate partitions
do not affect its validity. Conditioning on membership of Omega is
already implicit. Refining all its existing coordinates for free, without
charging their cells, is not the factor specified in the energy lemma.

## 4. Uniform discrepancy by a probability argument

Write n=2^d, k=floor(n/d), and a=k/n. Independently include each point
of Omega with probability a. For a cell S of size N_S, its occupied
count Z_S has mean a N_S. For any Bernoulli variable Y of mean a,

    log E exp(t(Y-a)) <= t^2/8,  t real.                    (5)

Here is a proof of the bound being used. The logarithm on the left and
its first derivative vanish at t=0. Its second derivative is the variance
of a tilted Bernoulli variable, at most 1/4. Integrating twice, for
positive or negative t, proves (5). Independence and the exponential
Markov inequality, optimized at t=4u/N_S, then give

    Pr(|Z_S-a N_S|>=u)<=2 exp(-2u^2/N_S).

In particular the probability of

    |Z_S-a N_S|>4d sqrt(N_S)                               (6)

is at most 2 exp(-32d^2). A union bound over (4) makes the probability
that any cell violates (6) at most 2T_d exp(-32d^2).

This is less than 1/[2(n+1)] for d>=2. For explicit constants, (1) gives
log p<6+5d<=8d. Since log d<=d, log(s+1)<=d, and s<=sqrt(d),

    log T_d <=2d+19d sqrt(d)<=21d^2,
    log(4(n+1))<=d+3<=2d^2.

Thus log(4(n+1)T_d)<=23d^2<32d^2, proving the asserted strict bound.

The total occupied count Z is Binomial(n,k/n). Its unique mode is k:
the ratio Pr(Z=j+1)/Pr(Z=j) decreases in j, is greater than one at
j=k-1 and less than one at j=k. Among its n+1 possible values,

    Pr(Z=k)>=1/(n+1).

Therefore the probability of size exactly k and no cell violating (6)
is positive. There is at least one actual k-point subset with all the
required discrepancy bounds simultaneously.

## 5. Deterministic choice and uniform energy bound

Order the n points of Omega increasingly. Define A_d as the
lexicographically first k-point subset satisfying, for every cell S in
the finite family from Section 3, the integer inequality

    (n |A_d intersect S|-k |S|)^2 <=16d^2 n^2 |S|.           (7)

All operations in this definition are finite integer operations: enumerate
characters, shifts t/(2bp), cells, and k-point subsets. Section 4 proves
that the search terminates. Thus A_d is a specified deterministic set,
not just a random variable or an assumed realizable probability table.

For a factor F with cells S, conditional expectation is the cell density,
so its variance is exactly

    sum_S (|S|/n)(|A_d intersect S|/|S|-a)^2.

By (7), each summand is at most 16d^2/n. Consequently, for any b<=d
and any factor with at most s characters,

    Var_Omega(E(1_A|F)) <=16d^2 b^s/2^d,
    Var_Omega(E(1_A|F))/a^2 <=64d^4 d^s/2^d ->0.            (8)

We used a>=1/(2d), since n/d>=2. The limit follows because
sqrt(d)log d+4log d=o(d).

For every fixed C, C log(2/a)<=C log(4d)<=floor(sqrt(d)) for all
sufficiently large d. Every factor requested in the unrestricted lemma
is therefore included in (8), eventually. For every fixed arc count b,
b<=d eventually too. This proves the simultaneous counterexample for
all fixed C and all fixed choices of the number of arcs. In particular,
no absolute eta>0 and C can satisfy the unrestricted energy lemma.

Since a=1/d+O(2^-d), n=2^d, and log p=d log64+O(1), the relative
density has exactly the logarithmic scale stated in Section 1.

## 6. Why this does not disprove the original target

The density a above is relative to Omega, not to the interval [p]. Its
global density obeys

    |A_d|/p_d <=1/(100d*32^d),

far below 1/log p_d. Relabeling the 2^d points of Omega as [2^d] does
not preserve arithmetic progressions. No dense interval counterexample
to r_4(N)=o(N/log N) has been constructed.

The previous cutoff-qualified energy lemma also remains OPEN. Indeed,
Omega itself is 3AP-free, so its AP count is entirely diagonal:

    C_Omega=Lambda_4(1_Omega)=n/p^2,
    beta_Omega=C_Omega/(n/p)^2=1/n.

The old sufficient cutoff n>=8*3^r*a^-3, r=rank(Omega), cannot hold.
More invariantly, its intended diagonal control is

    a^3 n beta_Omega >=8,                                  (9)

equivalent to aw/p<=a^4 C_Omega/8. Here the left side equals a^3<1.
This failure is independent of a redundant choice of rank representation.
Also Phi=a C_Omega, rather than approximately a^2 C_Omega; these examples
are not post-Phi-regularization instances.

Thus the literal Section-II statement in the latest request is FALSE.
If 'controlled window' was intended to include the earlier cutoff or
post-reduction assumptions, this family is outside that narrower class;
it does not settle that intended version. The missing hypothesis must
be restored explicitly before an attempted proof continues.

## 7. Replacement and the exact remaining obstruction

Further 14 September result: restoring only the scalar AP-availability
condition is insufficient. [AP_RICHNESS_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md](AP_RICHNESS_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md)
constructs a controlled window satisfying that condition by an arbitrarily
large margin, with superpolynomial-in-1/a effective direction support, yet
still violating uniform coarse energy. All its APs occur in a small
progression disjoint from A. The full old rank/size cutoff is not refuted.

The smallest repair excluding this construction is to restore AP
availability (9), or the original stronger explicit rank/size cutoff,
while preserving the already-proved hereditary and terminal accounting.
That repair has not been proved sufficient for the desired energy bound.

**Uniform coarse energy on AP-rich controlled windows — OPEN:** establish
absolute eta,C for the checkpoint-qualified windows and every eligible
4AP-free subset, with <=C log(2/a) quarter-arc character coordinates
capturing variance >=eta a^2. This must cover every branch and be uniform
through the corrected iteration of LOCALIZATION_BRIDGE.md.

It remains the single missing theorem for that route. A counting proof
must use the supply of nontrivial ambient APs; being a controlled window
and having logarithmic *relative* density are insufficient by themselves.

## 9. Factor/Fourier interpretation

For g=1_A-a1_Omega and a partition F of Omega into cells S, the exact
energy is

    Var_Omega(E(1_A|F))
      = sum_S |sum_{x in S}g(x)|^2/(n|S|)
      = (p^2/n) sum_S |sum_xi ghat(xi) conjugate(1_S_hat(xi))|^2/|S|.

The last equality is normalized Fourier Parseval. This is a quadratic
form involving every cell's Fourier coefficients, not the sum of Fourier
mass on the original selected characters. Quarter-arc indicators contain
harmonics, and intersections introduce their combinations. In Z_p the
unrestricted additive module generated by any nonzero character is the
whole dual group; a statement about a low-rank module needs a coefficient
range or quantization bound to have useful quantitative content. The
counterexample counts the entire quantized factors, including all of
their harmonics, rather than discarding them.

The preceding cutoff-qualified theorem and its stress tests are unchanged.
The Z_11 wrap, Z_101 mixed example, flat-spectrum sets, and concentrated
support examples neither prove nor refute the corrected AP-rich energy
lemma. The present construction directly satisfies the unrestricted new
statement's window and relative-density hypotheses, but deliberately does
not satisfy the old diagonal/AP-availability hypothesis. That distinction
is the reason its conclusion is false, not an omitted technicality.
