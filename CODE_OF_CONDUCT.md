# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in
this research project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Focusing on what is best for the mathematical argument
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Showing empathy toward other researchers
- Crediting prior work, including this repository

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information
- Stripping authorship or presenting this pack as your sole original
  closed work
- Claiming the problem is solved, or claiming a prize, without a
  complete public proof

## Enforcement

Report issues to the author, Mangesh Raut, at mbr63@drexel.edu or via
GitHub. Leaders will review and respond as they judge appropriate,
including warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban from the project
spaces they control.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
